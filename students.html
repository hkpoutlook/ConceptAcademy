What Makes a Great Learner?

  • Curiosity: Ask questions and explore your interests.
  • Collaboration: Work with peers to solve problems together.
  • Persistence: Don't give up—try again, learn from failure.
  • Critical Thinking: Analyze, question, and evaluate information.
  • Growth Mindset: Believe in your ability to learn and improve.
