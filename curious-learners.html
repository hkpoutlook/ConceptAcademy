Self-Directed Learning Toolkit

  • Ask Questions: What do you want to know? Start with real curiosity.
  • Research: Use libraries, credible websites, and expert sources.
  • Experiment: Test ideas, make mistakes, iterate.
  • Reflect: What did you learn? How does it connect to what you knew?
  • Share: Teach others, write, create—make your learning visible.

Community & Collaboration
