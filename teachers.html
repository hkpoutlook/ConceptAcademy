Our Teaching Philosophy

  • Active Learning: Students learn by doing, not just listening.
  • Evidence-Based: Methods grounded in educational research and proven outcomes.
  • Differentiation: Meet students where they are; personalize pathways.
  • Critical Thinking: Teach reasoning, analysis, and problem-solving alongside content.
  • Collaboration: Build community in the classroom; students learn from each other.

Grade-Specific Teaching Guides

Elementary (Grades 1–6)

Focus on foundational skills, wonder, and joy in learning. Build reading fluency, numeracy, and scientific inquiry. Use play-based learning and multisensory approaches.

Middle School (Grades 7–9)

Develop independence, abstract thinking, and research skills. Encourage collaboration, peer teaching, and exploration of identities and interests.

High School (Grades 10–12)

Prepare for higher education and careers. Offer electives, AP/IB, independent projects, and mentorship. Build leadership and global perspectives.
